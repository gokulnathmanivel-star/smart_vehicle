How SOS Breakdown Assistance Operates

When stranded on the road, our automated geospatial dispatch engine connects you with the nearest qualified mechanic in seconds.

1
Trigger 1-Tap SOS

Press the SOS button. Your browser Geolocation captures exact coordinates and sends an emergency distress signal.

2
Smart Haversine Dispatch

Our dispatch engine calculates great-circle distances and assigns the closest available mechanic with the right tools.

3
Roadside Fix & Digital Bill

Mechanic arrives on-site, rectifies the breakdown, logs parts used, and generates an instant digital invoice.
